# Mobile UX research, 15 September 2026

Three research passes commissioned for the design overhaul proposal
(`docs/product/proposal-2026-09/nouns-before-apps.html`). Condensed; every
claim carries its source.

## 1. Phone shells

- **iOS 26.** Liquid Glass reviewed as mixed (text distortion under glass, transparency fails on varied backgrounds) — https://sixcolors.com/post/2025/09/ios-26-review-through-a-glass-liquidly/ . Live Activities are the strongest idea: one state object rendered on Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, StandBy, CarPlay — https://9to5mac.com/2025/12/04/ios-26-made-live-activities-even-better-on-iphone-heres-whats-new/ , https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/223/ . Notification summaries and Focus — https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/summarize-notifications-reduce-interruptions-iph1fbe7d2b9/ios .
- **Android 16 / Pixel.** Material 3 Expressive; drawer as sheet — https://9to5google.com/2025/09/03/android-16-qpr1-pixel/ . Live Updates eligibility rule (ongoing, user-initiated, time-sensitive; not chat/ads/passive tracking) — https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/notifications/live-update . Themed monochrome third-party icons reversed for legibility. Gemini overlay absorbed Circle to Search — https://9to5google.com/2026/05/11/gemini-overlay-circle-search/ .
- **Nothing OS 3/4.** One typographic voice (Ndot + mono grid), monochrome icon pack, 18 first-party widgets — https://m.gsmarena.com/nothing_phone_3a-review-2811p4.php , https://www.androidauthority.com/nothing-os-3-hands-on-3488739/ . Essential Key/Space — https://www.engadget.com/mobile/smartphones/nothing-phone-3a-essential-spaces-ai-powered-app-done-right-163042976.php . OS 4 Extra Dark, Playground — https://gadgets.beebom.com/guides/best-nothing-os-4-0-features . Design system as documented by third parties — https://www.shadcn.io/design/nothing , https://github.com/dominikmartn/nothing-design-skill .
- **Light Phone III.** Tools not apps; vertical list; no notifications by design — https://analogbag.net/blog/light-phone-iii-review , https://www.consumerreports.org/electronics-computers/cell-phones/light-phone-3-review-a1105801271/ .
- **Punkt MC02.** Greyscale without typographic rigor looks unfinished; the "Ledger" permission dials are the good idea — https://www.slashgear.com/1548806/punkt-mc02-review-apostrophy-grapheneos-android-privacy-phone-not-great/ .
- **webOS** (cards, Stacks, Just Type, Synergy) — https://www.engadget.com/2010-10-19-webos-2-0-review.html . **Windows Phone Metro** (content before chrome, Live Tiles, hubs by noun, pin a person) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metro_(design_language) . **BlackBerry 10** (Hub, Peek) — https://www.pocket-lint.com/phones/news/blackberry/117427-blackberry-10-detailed-flow-hub/ . **Sailfish** gestures — https://sailfishos.org/design/gestures/ . **KaiOS** softkeys — https://developer.kaiostech.com/docs/getting-started/main-concepts/softkeys/ .
- **Minimal launchers.** Niagara (vertical list, inline notifications, summary) — https://niagaralauncher.com/niagara-pro-features/ ; Olauncher — https://maketecheasier.com/olauncher-minimalist-android-launcher/ ; Before Launcher — https://beforelabs.com/ ; Blloc Ratio — https://9to5google.com/2020/05/06/blloc-ratio-hands-on/ ; survey — https://treelauncher.app/blog/complete-guide-minimalist-launchers-2026.html .

Principles drawn: typography is the identity; vertical lists over grids; one state object, many surfaces; strict eligibility for ambient attention; aggregate by noun not app; peek before commit; type to act; notifications next to their subject; content before chrome; make usage visible; curated toggles beat stores. Pitfalls: translucency over content; monochrome third-party icons; auto-categorized drawers; unbounded "ongoing" chips; a capture key with no other input; expressive motion as identity; minimalism without an escape hatch.
